# Competitors

## AI Journaling
- **Reflectly** — AI-guided journaling app. Treats entries as GDPR special category data. Structured reflection sessions.
- **Rosebud** — AI journal using OpenAI/Anthropic/Groq. Strong privacy model: anonymizes entries before AI, Zero Data Retention agreements with all providers.
- **Day One** — Premium journaling app (Automattic). Gold standard for privacy: end-to-end encryption, user-controlled keys, cannot read entries. No AI features.

## Meditation & Mindfulness
- **Headspace** — Meditation and mental wellness. Has AI companion (Ebb). HIPAA-compliant. Broad content license over user data.
- **Calm** — Meditation, sleep, relaxation. Large content library. Retains data as long as "necessary."
- **Waking Up (Sam Harris)** — Meditation and philosophical inquiry. More intellectual/philosophical angle. Offers refunds for dissatisfaction or financial hardship.
- **Plum Village** — Thich Nhat Hanh's community app. Simple, spiritual, non-commercial feel. Uses Firebase analytics. Inspiration for Pania's landing page aesthetic.

## Notes
- Day One and Rosebud are the closest privacy benchmarks for Pania's architecture
- Plum Village is the closest aesthetic/spiritual benchmark
- No direct competitor combines AI journaling with multi-tradition spiritual wisdom the way Pania does
